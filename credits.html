其他模型及早期保留素材

Spitfire（早期保留，当前未使用）

Spitfire from Ultimate Spaceships Pack by Quaternius (CC0). Converted via the Malcolm Nixon asset port.

作者与素材来源 · CC0-1.0

Converted Blender source to standalone GLB; rotated 180 degrees about up axis for -Z forward; official blue livery selected; material metalness .45 and roughness .38.

Car Concept（早期保留，当前未使用）

Car Concept by Eric Chadwick / Darmstadt Graphics Group GmbH, 2024, based on a public-domain concept by Unity Fan. CC BY 4.0. Adapted for this project: graphite/silver materials, normalized wheel pivots, centered/grounded hierarchy, sample logo surfaces removed.

作者与素材来源 · CC-BY-4.0

Flattened static hierarchy while preserving world geometry. Rebuilt wheel_fl/fr/rl/rr pivots with local X axle and local Y steering. Grounded tires and centered length. Graphite/silver paint and dark interior material colors applied preserving PBR maps. Replaced license plate with blank material; removed steering emblem; removed logo-bearing tire sidewall color and normal maps.

Service Pistol

Service Pistol by Mateusz Sadek / Poly Haven (CC0). One assembled wood-grip variant adapted for game use.

作者与素材来源 · CC0-1.0

Selected wood-grip variant and its slide, hammer, trigger. Assembled empty magazine into grip. Removed duplicate taped variant, loose loaded magazine and loose bullet. Rotated muzzle to -Z. Packed textures into GLB.

玩具公园 · 当前自由驾驶世界

红色遥控越野车、橙色展览世界、立体字母、玩具与展示物件均为本课程 Demo 程序化建模。整体视觉与探索交互参考 Bruno Simon 2019 作品，使用本项目独立制作的几何与控制代码。此世界没有任务、倒计时与通关要求。

早期「松风原野」版本参考 art of rally 的微缩构图，现已由玩具公园替代。
